Q14. Compare HBase and Cassandra for a real-time analytics platform.

Requirements: high write volume, large datasets, geographically distributed users and occasional node failures.

HBase offers strong consistency for normal operations, very large-scale storage, Hadoop ecosystem integration, region-based scaling and HDFS fault tolerance.

Cassandra offers peer-to-peer architecture, high availability, replication, tunable consistency, geographic distribution and strong write scalability.

For this scenario, Cassandra is generally the stronger choice because availability, geographic distribution, high write volume and tolerance of node failures are central requirements.

However, the choice assumes that analytics queries can be modeled effectively using Cassandra's partition and clustering design. If deep Hadoop integration or HBase-specific storage/consistency characteristics were the priority, HBase could be preferable.
