Q8. Explain major query features of HBase and Cassandra.

HBase operations include:
- Put: insert/update data.
- Get: retrieve a row or cells.
- Scan: read a range of rows.
- Delete: remove data.
- Increment: update numeric values in supported cases.

Cassandra uses CQL (Cassandra Query Language) with INSERT, SELECT, UPDATE and DELETE.

Example:
SELECT * FROM students WHERE student_id = 'S101';

CQL resembles SQL but is designed for Cassandra's distributed model and does not provide unrestricted relational querying.

Query design and data modeling are especially important because column-family databases are query-driven. Poor row/partition keys can create hotspots, while queries that require huge scans can be expensive. Data may be denormalized to make important queries fast.
