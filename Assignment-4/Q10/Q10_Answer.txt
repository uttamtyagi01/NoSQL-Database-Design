Q10. Design a real-time event logging system.

A system producing millions of events per minute needs high write throughput, horizontal scalability, large storage, fault tolerance and fast retrieval.

Conceptual table: Events

Partition/row key:
<service_id>#<time_bucket>#<shard>

Columns:
event:event_id
event:type
event:timestamp
event:user_id
event:message
metadata:device
metadata:location
metadata:source

Example key:
API1#2026-10-06-10#03

Time buckets limit partition/region size and shards distribute heavy writes.

Consistency should match the business requirement. Logging may tolerate tunable/eventual-style consistency when durability and availability are more important than immediate global consistency.

Use replication for availability, multiple shards to prevent hotspots, time buckets for bounded partitions and horizontal node scaling as traffic grows.
