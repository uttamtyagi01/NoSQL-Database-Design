Q11. Explain Graph Databases and their data model.

A graph database stores highly connected information using nodes and relationships.

1. Nodes:
Represent entities such as Person, Product, Student or Company.

2. Relationships/Edges:
Represent connections between nodes, such as FRIENDS_WITH, PURCHASED, WORKS_AT or ENROLLED_IN.

3. Properties:
Key-value attributes stored on nodes or relationships.

4. Labels:
Categories/types assigned to nodes, such as :Person, :Product or :Student.

Example:
(:Person {name:'Aman'})-[:FRIENDS_WITH]->(:Person {name:'Riya'})

Graph databases are suitable for highly connected data because relationships are stored as first-class elements. Queries can traverse connections directly instead of repeatedly joining many relational tables.

Common applications:
- Social networks.
- Recommendation systems.
- Fraud detection.
- Knowledge graphs.
- Network analysis.
- Dependency analysis.
