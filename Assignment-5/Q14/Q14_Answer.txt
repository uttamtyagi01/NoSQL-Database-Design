Q14. Model a social networking application using Neo4j.

Main nodes:
(:Person)
(:Post)
(:Group)

Possible relationships:
(:Person)-[:FRIENDS_WITH]->(:Person)
(:Person)-[:FOLLOWS]->(:Person)
(:Person)-[:CREATED]->(:Post)
(:Person)-[:LIKES]->(:Post)
(:Person)-[:MEMBER_OF]->(:Group)

Example:
(:Person {id:'U01',name:'Aman'})
  -[:FRIENDS_WITH]->
(:Person {id:'U02',name:'Riya'})

Post:
(:Person)-[:CREATED]->(:Post {id:'P101',text:'Hello'})

Like:
(:Person)-[:LIKES]->(:Post)

Group:
(:Person)-[:MEMBER_OF]->(:Group {id:'G01',name:'Data Analysts'})

Recommendation queries can traverse relationships such as:
A user's friends -> their liked posts -> related users/content.

Advantages:
1. Direct representation of relationships.
2. Efficient multi-hop traversal.
3. Natural representation of followers and friendships.
4. Useful for recommendations.
5. Easy pattern-based relationship queries.
6. Flexible addition of new relationship types.

Neo4j is therefore well suited to social applications where relationships are as important as the user data itself.
